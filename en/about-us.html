About us

A story that began over a beer,
and continues with care.

Two friends, two beds
and one garage

Behind the evobeds brand are lifelong friends Viktor Pokorný and Radim Čech. Their journey began not in a boardroom but over a beer, as they complained about how impossible it was to get a positioning bed into the home for their unwell grandparents. So they decided to do something about it.

How it began

Can't buy one? Borrow one. Better still, we'll lend you one.

Both men were caring for their grandparents at the time, and both discovered that getting an ordinary positioning bed into the home was an almost superhuman task. The big manufacturers wouldn't deal with individuals. So each of them bought a bed with their own money, and together they opened a rental service.

Their office was a spare room and their warehouse a rented garage. They drove the beds to customers' homes, assembled them on the spot and taught families how to use them. That was unusual at the time, but to them it came naturally, because they had started out in exactly the same situation themselves.

How we grew

The garage grew into Invira.
Invira grew into a bigger dream.

Renting naturally grew into selling, because customers themselves asked for it. Today Invira, the founders' medical equipment company, has its headquarters and warehouses in Ostrava, helps thousands of families every year and exports across Europe. Yet its approach is the same as it was at the start:

  • We deliver, assemble and demonstrate everything in your home
  • You can try the equipment for a month and swap it if needed
  • If you buy, we deduct the rental fee from the price
  • We refurbish older equipment for people who can't afford new

Milestones

From two beds
to a brand of our own

2011

An idea over a beer, and the first two positioning beds bought with their own money. A rental service for the public is born.

2012

Invira s.r.o. is founded, offering sales, rental and servicing of medical equipment across the Czech Republic and Slovakia.

2020

The experience gained from thousands of families leads to evobeds One, our own smart positioning bed, created in collaboration with a Czech designer.

2022

evobeds expands into other European markets. Beds made in the Czech Republic begin serving families and facilities abroad.

Where we are today

The bed we wished we had
for our own loved ones

Today, evobeds looks after the brand, sales and customer care, while Invira handles production, servicing and logistics. Every bed is created through collaboration between developers, healthcare professionals and caring families, because we know exactly what it means to care for someone close to you.
