What happens next. Four steps to a bed at your home.

  1. 1

    We confirm your order

    Right after submitting you will receive an order summary with your configuration by e-mail. Our sales team gets a copy too and will contact you within two working days to go through everything with you.

  2. 2

    We send the payment details

    After confirming the order we will send you a proforma invoice with all payment details, meaning the account number, the reference number and the amount.

  3. 3

    Your bed goes into production

    As soon as the payment is credited, we hand the order over to production in the Czech Republic. Every bed is inspected and tested by hand before dispatch.

  4. 4

    We deliver and assemble

    We arrange the delivery date by phone. We assemble the bed right in the bedroom, set up the positions, demonstrate the controls and take the packaging away.
