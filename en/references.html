Case studies

How evobeds serves
in real facilities

Every installation begins with a personal presentation and a trial run right at your facility. See what rooms with evobeds beds look like in facilities across the country.

Sanatorium

Sanatorium Libella

Starting point

The sanatorium needed beds that could handle everyday long-term care without turning the rooms into a hospital environment.

Why evobeds

What decided it was the look of a home bed with the functions of a medical bed, and removable washable covers that make room hygiene easier.

How it works today

The staff use the electric height adjustment in everyday care and the rooms have kept the homely atmosphere that clients and families appreciate.

Care home

Dobrá Voda Spa Care Home

Starting point

The home was renewing its room furnishings and looking for beds suitable even for clients with limited mobility, without the rooms losing their cosiness.

Why evobeds

The upholstered design matches the home's interior and the four-section adjustable surface covers clients' needs from rest to safe standing up.

How it works today

The beds serve in daily operation and the 24 hour service gives the home certainty that care will never wait for technology.

Care home

Tmaň Care Home

Starting point

The home was gradually replacing older medical beds and wanted the new rooms to feel like home, not like a ward.

Why evobeds

Folding, removable side rails integrated into the upholstery provide safety without an institutional feel, and the cover colours could be matched to the interior.

How it works today

Rooms furnished with evobeds are among the first the home shows to families on tours.

Senior residence

Daczická Residence, Kutná Hora

Starting point

A residence focused on a hotel-standard environment was looking for beds that would make no compromise between care and aesthetics.

Why evobeds

Premium upholstery and thoughtful details fit the residence's concept, and the technology stays hidden inside the bed.

How it works today

The beds are a natural part of the rooms and give clients independence in standing up and positioning.

Care home

Chodová Planá Care Home

Starting point

The home was replacing beds in rooms with round-the-clock care and needed to make positioning clients easier for the staff.

Why evobeds

Quiet motors and smooth height adjustment spare caregivers' backs, and both staff and clients can operate the bed with a single button.

How it works today

The beds are in daily use and the home works with us on adding accessories according to individual clients' needs.

Care home

Mělník Care Home

Starting point

While modernising its rooms, the home wanted to increase client comfort and unify the equipment so the staff could work the same way everywhere.

Why evobeds

Unified controls, washable covers and the option to join two beds into a double give the home flexibility in assigning rooms.

How it works today

evobeds beds serve across the rooms and the home recommends them to colleagues from other facilities.
