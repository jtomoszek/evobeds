How the bed helps

Care has moved home.
The hospital stayed behind the door.

A loved one comes back from hospital. Getting up is harder every day. Care at home is suddenly a daily routine. We know these moments, and we know the last thing you need is a bed that turns the bedroom into a hospital room.

Dignity at home

A bed that does not remind you something has changed

evobeds looks like a modern upholstered bed, and that is exactly how the person lying in it sees it too. No cold frame, no stigma of a medical aid. Home stays home, even when it becomes a place of care.

  • Upholstered design in ten colours
  • Technology hidden inside the bed
  • Castors and details that match your furniture

Safer care

Your back is a caregiver too. Spare it.

Electric height adjustment raises the bed to a comfortable working position, so changing, hygiene and positioning happen without bending. And when it is time to get up, the bed safely sits your loved one up and helps them to their feet.

  • Height adjustment at the touch of a button
  • A position for easy and safe standing up
  • Pressure relief mattress for long-term lying
  • Washable cover, hygiene without compromise
